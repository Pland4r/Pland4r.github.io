MA CASES — LAUNCH DAY

Do not post into an empty profile. Fill the grid first so the first visitor
sees a shop, then start the Reels.

MORNING    Create the account on your own phone. Switch to Business.
           Set name, bio, link, profile picture, category.

+30 MIN    Post 3 stills back to back — GT3 RS, Senna, M4 CSL.
           Car name only. This is the grid, not the launch.

+1 HR      Build the 5 highlights. Even near-empty ones make the profile
           look run rather than abandoned.

12:30      FIRST REEL: the R34, full caption.
           This is the real launch — the widest-recognition car you own.
           captions/01-nissan-skyline-gt-r-r34-1.txt

ALL DAY    Story every hour. Behind the scenes, cases in your hand, a poll.
           Stories tell Instagram the account is live.

EVENING    Send the Reel to everyone you know and ask them to send it on.
           Sends on day one are worth more than any hashtag.

------------------------------------------------------------------ before all this

Set a price and a WhatsApp number in data/site.ts. Right now the link in your
bio lands on "price on request" and the order button is switched off. A shop
that will not say a price loses most of the people who clicked.
