------------------------------------------------------------------ no marques

No BMW, Porsche, GT-R or Bugatti in the bio. Those names belong to their
manufacturers, and a shop selling unofficial cases does not want them in the
one field that reads as a claim about the business itself.

They stay where they are a description rather than a claim: in the captions,
which say what is printed on a particular case, and in the hashtags, which is
also where the search traffic they bring actually lands.

------------------------------------------------------------------ searchability

The Name field is the other thing Instagram search reads, so it carries the
generic words instead:

  MA Cases | Coques auto

Someone typing "coques auto" or "coques maroc" finds you through that, with no
marque name anywhere on the profile.

------------------------------------------------------------------ why French

The bio is read once, by someone deciding whether to follow, and French reads
as a shop. The captions are read every day, and Darija outperforms French
there.
