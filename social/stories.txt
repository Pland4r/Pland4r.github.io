MA CASES — STORIES

Two or three a day. Stories do not win new followers; they keep the ones you
have, and they are where orders actually get asked for.

------------------------------------------------------------------ what to post

1. QUESTION STICKER — tells you what to stock next, and every reply is an open DM
2. POLL, two cases side by side — costs nothing, people always tap
3. THE PACKING STORY — film wrapping one order. The most convincing thing you can post
4. REPOST THE REEL an hour after it goes up, with «جديد 🔥»
5. COUNTDOWN 24h before a new case lands — Instagram notifies the people who tap
6. SCREENSHOT A GOOD DM, name covered, with permission
